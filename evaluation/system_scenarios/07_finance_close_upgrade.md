# Finance close upgrade

Create a database with the milestone-6 application, start reporting, and build a report containing
cash and hotel-credit movements. Upgrade that database with milestone 7 and close the period.

Record an old-dated chargeback after the close. Require the published report to remain identical,
the correction to appear on the first open day with its signed late-adjustment classifications,
and both reports and durable operation retries to remain identical after a process restart.
